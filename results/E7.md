# E7: speech, Omnilingual ASR (2026-10-22; Punjabi not run)

Procedure fixed in the amendment of 22 October before any clip was scored. Run on a Modal L4,
both models on the card, batches of at most 30 s of audio, no clip lost to GPU memory. Scorer
`eval/e7.py`; numbers only (Common Voice terms).

## Run so far

Done, in the pre-registered order: Urdu dev, Urdu test, Saraiki test, Sindhi test, Pashto test.
**Not run yet:** Punjabi test (the call sent all 84 long clips at once and hit Modal's 30-minute limit;
nothing was returned), then Saraiki, Sindhi, Pashto and Punjabi dev, and Pashto without a language code.
Modal cost so far $1.55 (billing report), of which about $0.40 was the timed-out Punjabi call.

## Abstention (dev sets run 22 October after the order amendment; thresholds set on dev, measured once on test)

| Language | Dev clips | Threshold (two-model disagreement) | Test clips accepted | 7B mean CER on accepted | 7B mean CER on all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urdu | 267 | 0.208 | 298 of 299 (99.7%) | 4.92% | 4.91% |
| Sindhi | 200 | 0.069 | 158 of 200 (79%) | 4.77% | 7.17% |
| Pashto | 217 | 0.000 | 4 of 200 (2%) | 3.55% | 13.16% |
| Saraiki | 200 | none reaches 5% on dev | 0 of 1,037 | none accepted | 21.99% |

- **Urdu prediction (at most 5% on accepted clips while accepting at least 80%): met, but not because of
  abstention.** Urdu is under 5% on all clips; the threshold removes one clip.
- **Sindhi:** the refusal works as intended: accepting the 79% of clips where both models agree brings CER
  from 7.17% to 4.77%.
- **Pashto:** at the 5% bar only transcripts on which the two models agree exactly are accepted: 2%.
- **Saraiki:** no threshold meets 5% on dev, so, as pre-registered, nothing is accepted.
- For the product this means a spoken question in Saraiki or Pashto should be shown back to the user to
  confirm ("Did you say ...?") instead of acted on; a 5% bar is a choice made for this test, and a different
  bar would need its own test on fresh clips.
