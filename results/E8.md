Six IESCO bills (April to September 2026, protected domestic, 2 kW), a close-up and a casual phone
photo of each: 12 photos. Muse Spark Open read each photo once (reasoning "low"); none needed turning.
Answer key typed by Claude from the close-ups before the run; confirmed by Aashan on 22 October and re-checked by Claude
value by value against the photos the same day (all 115 match). One judgement call: June prints "Pro-Rata Consumption" but not
the longer "Pro-Rata based Present Reading" line; the key says yes, the model said no on the close-up. Cost $0.162.

## Verdicts, and what the run found wrong with SahiBill

As first run, **every photo got a false "snapshot shows no time stamp" problem**: the stamp on these
bills is small text over a halftone photo, the model returned nothing for it, and the code treated "not
read" as "not printed". Fixed after the run: only text actually read counts; a separate question asks
whether a stamp is printed at all (yes, no, cannot tell). Re-scored from the same readings (no new
calls): 11 of 12 photos raise no problem; their findings are "looks right" (due date, category, charges
and fuel adjustment where printed) or "cannot be determined" (billing period without the previous
reading date, time to pay without a delivery date, the April QTA rate, an unreadable meter photo).

**One false alarm remained (resolved below):** on the September close-up the model called the snapshot box blank, so
SahiBill says no meter photo is printed. The casual photo of the same bill read the meter photo as
3545, the billed reading. A finding that rests on something being absent needs more than one reading
before it is shown as a problem.

**Changed on 22 October at Aashan's request: ask instead of accuse.** When an absence comes only from
reading a photo (no meter photo, no stamp, no old-meter final reading), SahiBill now asks the user to
look, in English and Urdu, and alleges it only after the user confirms. Facts from labels or a confirmed
answer are unchanged, so E3 and E4 are unaffected. Re-scored from the same readings: **0 of 12 photos
raise a problem**; the September close-up gets the question "We could not see a meter-reading photo on
your bill. Please look: is there one?".

Two parser fixes after the run, disclosed: Urdu month names ("26-مئی") now parse; an unread stamp is no
longer evidence of a missing stamp. Both are tested; 205 tests pass after all of the changes above.
