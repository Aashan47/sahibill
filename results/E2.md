# E2: two readers with abstention (test half, 2026-10-22)

**Prediction (pre-registered):** among accepted fields, at most 1% wrong, while accepting at least 85%.
**Result: missed.** Coverage 85.5% (met); wrong among accepted 1.65% (not met). All 23 wrong accepted
fields are on damage level 3.

Data: the 100 test-half images (25 bills × levels 0 to 3), 1,632 printed fields. Settings frozen on
the calibration half before this run: Spark Open read once with straighten-and-reread (stage 2);
Glimmer 30B, prompt variant A, on Kaggle 2×T4 (run 5); a field is accepted only where both agree and
the consistency layer does not hold it. Scorer: `eval/e1.py`.

| Arm | Coverage | Wrong among accepted |
|---|---|---|
| Spark Open alone | 95.1% | 113 (7.28%) |
| Glimmer alone | 94.1% | 125 (8.14%) |
| Spark Open + consistency layer | 88.0% | 41 (2.86%) |
| **Spark Open + Glimmer + consistency (the pre-registered arm)** | **85.5%** | **23 (1.65%)** |

By damage level, pre-registered arm:

| Level | Fields | Coverage | Wrong among accepted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 408 | 100.0% | 0 |
| 1 | 408 | 100.0% | 0 |
| 2 | 408 | 97.8% | 0 |
| 3 | 408 | 44.4% | 23 (12.71%) |

What the errors are: 23 fields on 13 level-3 images, mostly money amounts (fixed 5, QTA 4, payable 2,
variable 2, FCA 2) and single blurred digits read the same wrong way by both models (5 as 3, 9 as 0,
6 as 8). Two models agreeing does not catch an error both make; the second reader halves the wrong
accepted fields (41 to 23) at a cost of 2.5 points of coverage.

Off-schema output: Glimmer returned 2 of 100 images outside the schema (one cut off at the 3,000-token
cap, one field given as a bare value). Both are treated as unread, so they are held; this lowers
coverage and cannot raise accuracy. The same rule is now in `agree.combine` and in the scorer.

Speed: Glimmer took a median 137 s per image on Kaggle's two T4s (3.9 hours for 100 images), so live use
needs a faster GPU.

Nothing here was tuned on the test half. Any change prompted by this result (for example a photo
quality gate that asks for a retake before reading a level-3 photo) must be set on the calibration half
or fresh images and tested on images it has not seen.
