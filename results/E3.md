# E3 result: finding seeded defects on synthetic bills, through the product path

Run once on 21 October 2026 on the frozen stage-2 readings of the 100 test-half images: Spark Open reading,
the consistency layer, then the rules.

## Pre-registered result

| Damage level | Seeded defects the bill can show | Found | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 26 | 20 | 76.9% |
| 1 | 26 | 20 | 76.9% |
| 2 | 26 | 20 | 76.9% |
| 3 | 26 | 16 | 61.5% |

Prediction: each check finds at least 95% of its seeded defects. **Not met.** The false-flag rate on clean
bills **could not be measured**: the generator placed every clean bill in the calibration half, so the test
half has none.

## Diagnosis, post hoc (no code or score was changed after seeing the test results)

Most misses and the extra flags trace to the synthetic generator's answer key, not to the reading or the
rules:

- Detection months exceeded (2 of 2 missed at level 2): the synthetic detection line prints no reasons and
  no approval. Under the written rule (spec case DM-02) 4 to 6 months without them is cannot_be_determined,
  which the engine returned. The answer key called it determinable.
- Detection against history (1 of 1): the comparison months, a year earlier, fall outside the 12-month
  history printed on the bill (spec DH-04). Answer key flaw.
- Late retrieval (2 of 2): the generator never prints the defect type; CSM 4.3.2(d) covers display-wash
  defects only (spec RT-10). Answer key flaw.
- Long period not prorated (1 of 2 missed at level 2): in one bill the seeded defect changed nothing (a
  protected household stayed in its slab); in another the reader did not return the printed previous
  reading date, a genuine reading miss.
- Extra tariff_arithmetic_error flags on 7 of 25 level-2 bills: the generator computed the fuel charge
  adjustment on a month's units before rewriting that month in the printed history, so the printed bill is
  internally inconsistent and the engine was right to flag it.

These are fixed in the generator for future sets. Re-measuring E3 needs a fresh synthetic set read again,
which is a new paid run.

## Fixed after E3 (affects future sets only; the frozen test set and the result above are unchanged)

The generator now builds each bill in a fixed order (history and history-changing defects, then category
and charges, then billing defects, then month-based adjustments), prints detection reasons and approval
status, prints the defect type and retrieval date on retrieval lines, places detection periods so the
months after them are on the bill, drops a seeded defect that changes nothing, and puts clean bills in both
halves. A new test runs the rules on a perfect reading of every generated bill and requires exactly the
seeded violations and nothing else: it passes on 180 bills from three seeds.
